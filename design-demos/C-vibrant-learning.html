How Free Is Your Thinking?

Critical thinking is the process of evaluating ideas and information to reach well-founded conclusions. It requires us to question our assumptions, examine evidence carefully, and remain open to perspectives that differ from our own.

The undisciplined mind accepts whatever it encounters; the disciplined mind tests each claim before embracing it. To think well is not to be clever, but to be honest about what we do not yet know.

Many of our opinions are not really ours at all. They were absorbed, uncritically, from the surrounding culture before we could scrutinize them.
